# Leo Ruesche Neggia

**Climate and energy policy · Development · Evidence synthesis**

I am a master’s student in the dual-degree programme between
Sciences Po and Peking University, studying International Development
and International Relations.

My interests span environmental and development economics, with
a focus on how public policies can support effective and equitable
climate action. My research experience includes systematic reviews,
meta-analysis, and comparative policy analysis.

## Research interests

- Climate and energy policy evaluation
- Community energy and energy-efficient technology adoption
- Sufficiency and sustainable development
- International development and South–South cooperation

## Selected research

### Energy-efficient appliance adoption

Research comparing the effects of behavioral, informational, and
monetary interventions on energy-efficient appliance adoption.

Co-authored paper published in *PNAS Nexus*.

### Community energy in Europe

Comparative analysis of policy and regulatory frameworks supporting
community energy across eight European countries.

First-authored manuscript under review, with a preprint available.

### Sufficiency policies in Paris

Research evaluating 58 municipal policy instruments across 13 sectors
using a six-pillar sufficiency framework.

Manuscript in preparation.

## Policy experience and engagement

My experience includes research programme coordination at the
Centre for Economic Policy Research, data analysis at the OECD,
and climate advocacy and organisational leadership at CliMates
International.

I have also contributed to guest lectures and public discussions
on climate, development, and a just transition.
