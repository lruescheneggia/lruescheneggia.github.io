# Research

My research interests span climate and energy policy evaluation,
environmental and development economics, and evidence synthesis.

## Publications

### Energy-efficient appliance adoption

Khanna T. M., Danilenko D., Tomberg L., Hansteen S., Andor M. A.,
Ruesche Neggia L., and Minx J. C. (2026).

*Meta-analysis on comparative impact of behavioral, information,
and monetary interventions on energy-efficient appliance adoption.*

Published in **PNAS Nexus**.

This research compares interventions aimed at encouraging the
adoption of energy-efficient appliances. My contribution included
abstract and full-text coding and contributions to the manuscript.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgag129)

## Under review

### Policy frameworks for community energy

Ruesche Neggia L., Amer M., O’Dogherty Tábora Solares L.,
Nsude C., and Khanna T. M. (2026).

*Policy frameworks for community energy: lessons from a
cross-country comparative analysis in Europe.*

Under review at **Energy Policy**. Preprint available on SSRN.

This research examines policy and regulatory frameworks across
eight European countries to understand the conditions supporting
community energy initiatives and community-based virtual power plants.

My contribution included comparing national frameworks and
synthesising academic literature across seven analytical dimensions.

[Read the preprint](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7593758)

## Work in progress

### Effectiveness of climate mitigation policies

*Systematic review and meta-analysis of ex-post evaluations
on the effectiveness of global climate mitigation policies.*

Manuscript in preparation, with a multi-author research team.

This project evaluates evidence on the effectiveness of climate
mitigation policies across five sectors. My contribution included
review protocol development and abstract and full-text screening.

### Sufficiency policies in Paris

Ruesche Neggia L., Girard M., and Saheb Y.

*Systematically evaluating Parisian municipal policies using
a 6-pillar sufficiency framework.*

Manuscript in preparation.

This project examines 58 municipal policy instruments across
13 sectors in Paris. My contribution included identifying and
evaluating policies, building and analysing the policy dataset,
and synthesising findings for policymakers.
